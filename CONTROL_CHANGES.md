# Relay controls — temporal rework

Main-hand Relay: right-click Time Dilate; sneak + right-click Temporal Divinity; ordinary left-click Rewind. Passive Tuned In has no input. Offhand Relay grants passives/charge; ordinary sword use and F remain unchanged. Separate cooldowns are shown on the existing mythic HUD.

This replaces the old offhand hold and direct-hit Time Lock. See CHANGELOG.md for migration, limitations, default balance and unrun multiplayer checks.

## Inside Temporal Divinity

Caster only: W/S swim toward/away from where you look (up and down included), A/D strafe, jump rises, sneak dives (after releasing the sneak used to cast), sprint surges forward, releasing all keys glides to a stop. Captured opponents have no movement controls: they sink slowly and cannot fly, glide, jump out or steer. See SWIM_UPDATE.md.
